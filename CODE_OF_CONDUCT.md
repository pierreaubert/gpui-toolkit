# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.
Contributors and maintainers should be constructive, assume good faith, accept
technical feedback gracefully, respect boundaries, and avoid discriminatory,
threatening, sexualized, or personally demeaning conduct.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other
project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct harms the
community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, private where
possible, and focused on safety rather than public punishment.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile or another maintainer you trust. Do not use a public issue when doing
so would expose the reporter or affected people. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, subject to legal and safety obligations.

This concise policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
